last updated: october 17, 2026

skinSniper (skinsniper.xyz) lets you track Counter-Strike 2 skin prices and get notified when they hit a threshold you set. This policy explains what data we collect to make that work, why, and what rights you have over it under the GDPR.

1. Who's responsible for your data

skinSniper is the data controller for the information below. Questions or requests go to contact@skinsniper.xyz.

2. What we collect

  • Discord ID and username - from Discord OAuth when you sign in. We never see your email or password.
  • IP address, browser, and OS - logged at sign-in to catch alt accounts; not used for anything else.
  • Tracked skins and price thresholds - whatever you set up in the tracker, tied to your Discord ID.
  • One session cookie - keeps you signed in. No analytics, no ad trackers.

3. Why we process it, and on what basis

We use this data to run your account, operate the tracker and notifications, and prevent abuse like multi-accounting. Under GDPR Art. 6, account and tracker data is processed to perform our contract with you; the sign-in security data is processed under our legitimate interest in keeping the Service abuse-free. We don't use your data for advertising and never sell it.

4. Who we share it with

Discord (sign-in), Supabase (database hosting), and Cloudflare (hosting/security) process data on our behalf to run the Service. We also query the Steam Community Market for public prices, but send it nothing about you. None of these get to use your data for their own purposes.

5. How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Email us and we'll delete your Discord ID, username, tracked skins, and sign-in logs.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, ask us to restrict or stop processing it, get a copy in a portable format, and complain to your local data protection authority if you think we've gotten it wrong. Reach out to contact@skinsniper.com for any of this.

7. Under 13s

skinSniper isn't intended for anyone under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has given us data, let us know and we'll remove it.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Keep using skinSniper after a change and that means you're fine with the update.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at contact@skinsniper.xyz.
